Controls
Move with WASD / arrows (the mouse never moves you) · Z: sneak (slower, quieter, harder to spot; Z again to stand up) · hold right-click to aim — the crosshair follows your cursor, you face its side, and left-click fires toward it (auto/semi guns fire while held; R reloads, and so does a click on an empty magazine) · left-click to interact (open, pick up, pick a lock); hold left-click for the full action list · F or Space uses what is towards the cursor · mouse wheel or 1–9, 0: choose what you hold from the belt (a weapon is ready at once; a grenade is thrown where you click; food and meds are used with a click) · I or C: inventory and crafting (next to a workbench its recipes show there too) · B: build bar (wheel over the kinds, click one, then pick a blueprint and click it to take it in hand; click the ground to place, F to work on it) · X: remove what you built · R with a chest or a fire open: take everything out
